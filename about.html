About Silver Labs

Silver Labs Technology & Development develops the Silver Labs ecosystem: Silver Pool, Silver Scan, SilverPay, SilverForge, SilvEx and BTCS L2 research. We began with Bitcoin Silver, but the broader platform is designed for multiple coins, networks and developers.

Independent product identity

Silver Labs' products are separate from the Bitcoin Silver organization's website and services. Silver Scan is Silver Labs' research terminal; it is not the Bitcoin Silver organization's explorer.

Development principles

Open project documentation, accurate feature-status labels, reproducible testing and explicit security boundaries. Early technical experiments and local verification must not be confused with production financial services.
