TECHNOLOGY / SYSTEM DESIGN

A connected ecosystem. Independent technology boundaries.

Silver Labs develops mining, market intelligence, self-custody, digital assets and exchange infrastructure. The architecture is intentionally modular: working experiments are identifiable, and integrations are not described as live until they have evidence.

01 / 4

Bitcoin Silver L1 and experimental L2

Bitcoin Silver L1 is the base blockchain. Silver Labs maintains separate node and explorer development and a BTCS L2 execution prototype. L2 currently has local Ethereum-compatible execution and anchor/recovery experiments, but still needs permissionless networking, fork choice, full settlement verification and production parameters.

BTCS L2 technical guide →Silver Scan explorer →
02 / 4

Mining and market data

Silver Pool provides a BTCS-first locally tested mining backend and intends to expand through independent algorithm/chain adapters. Silver Scan is a separate research service that can consume published pool analytics and chain data without running miners, holding funds or inventing statistics.

Silver Pool engineering →Silver Scan roadmap →
03 / 4

Wallets, asset creation and exchange

SilverPay is the user-controlled wallet track. SilverForge builds network-specific asset tooling; SilvEx and the DEX have separate trading and smart-contract engineering. One product's discovery page does not imply an approved wallet asset or exchange listing.

SilverPay wallet →SilverForge Studio →SilvEx exchange →DEX research →
04 / 4

Trust, data identity and security boundaries

Every asset must be identified by chain, network and contract/mint when appropriate. Price feeds, on-chain explorer observations, mining share ledgers, custody operations and advertising metrics have distinct security and provenance requirements. A verified data feed is not a guarantee of financial safety.
