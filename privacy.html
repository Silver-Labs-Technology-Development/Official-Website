Privacy notice — draft

This page describes the current static developer-preview site and requires review before it is treated as a final privacy policy.

Website data

The site's public project cards are static. This site does not provide account registration, a wallet login or a contact form. Hosting providers and GitHub may process technical access logs under their own policies.

External links

Following a GitHub link opens a third-party site governed by that provider's terms and privacy practices. Silver Scan's future Coin Watch prototype may store watched asset IDs in the browser itself; that is a separate product feature, not an account hosted by this website.

Changes

Any future analytics, contact forms, account features or backend services should be documented before deployment, including retention, rights requests and an appropriate contact channel.
