Frequently asked questions

Is Silver Labs the Bitcoin Silver organization?

No. Silver Labs operates its own products and developer repositories. Bitcoin Silver is a supported research and infrastructure starting point, not the boundary of every Silver Labs product.

Can I mine through Silver Pool today?

BTCS mining software is tested locally. Public TCP connectivity, miner share acceptance from an external connection and payout readiness must be verified before public miners are invited.

Does Silver Scan support every coin and chain?

No. Its BTCS L1 explorer has a development implementation. The research terminal is expanding into external coin markets and DEX pair discovery. It does not have complete multi-chain indexing, verified token listings or finalized market charts.

Are SilvEx trades, SilverPay payments or POW-S staking live?

Development is in progress, but no production trading, payment processing or staking rewards are claimed here.

Where can I follow progress?

Each project card on the ecosystem page links to its public code and documentation.
