02 / SILVER SCAN

Research, analytics and trustworthy data

Silver Scan is being developed to combine coin market research with blockchain activity and project profiles. Possible partner analytics include measured page views, referral clicks, promotional impressions and campaign trends, in addition to independently sourced market charts where providers permit.

03 / ECOSYSTEM

Multiple possible integration pathways

  • Silver Scan: profile discovery, independent market research, relevant on-chain data and measurable sponsored inventory.
  • Silver Pool: application for separate compatible proof-of-work network integration, requiring node and mining security tests.
  • SilverPay: future wallet/network consideration after independent security and interoperability verification.
  • SilverForge Studio: token and asset creation/deployment tooling where supported.
  • SilvEx: separate technical/compliance review for any future exchange listing. No automatic trading availability.
  • PoW-S: future eligible asset lockup design, subject to its own launch and legal/security review.
  • Soarin Media Group: creative services such as branding, articles, campaign artwork and social visuals.
