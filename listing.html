Listing information

Explore our proposed Silver Labs Project Growth program for sponsored billboards, project analytics, integrated discovery and long-term marketing support.

Silver Labs is designing a clear asset-listing review process for future SilvEx and research-platform integrations. Applications are not currently open, and no listing fee is being collected through this site.

Proposed review workflow

  1. Project submits exact blockchain, network, contract or mint address, token standard, team disclosures and official source links.
  2. Reviewers evaluate asset identity, technical compatibility, market integrity, security and applicable operational requirements.
  3. Any decision, deliverable and applicable fee is communicated in writing only after the relevant service is operational and approved.
  4. Silver Scan research visibility and a potential SilvEx trading listing are separate decisions; neither is automatic.

Proposed fee

A possible US$4,000 listing review fee has been discussed as a future business proposal. It is not an active price, payment request, guarantee of acceptance or current service offering. Formal fee terms, refunds and listing policies require review and publication before any applications or payments.
