SilvEx

Trading infrastructure built on verifiable markets and clear boundaries.

Current development statusDevelopment — trading interface onlySource-based overview · October 2026

SilvEx is Silver Labs' exchange development program. A trading terminal and read-only outside market-data adapters have been built, but this is not a live matching, custody or settlement service. External data must never appear as native SilvEx trading volume.

View development source ↗Contribute or collaborate
01 / ENGINEERING

What has already been built

  • An experimental trading interface with market charts and modular frontend work exists.
  • Read-only integrations with third-party market reference sources are present.
  • Backend authentication and operational safeguards remain active engineering priorities.
02 / PRODUCT

What the product is intended to provide

  • Market-discovery dashboards, accurate market identifiers, transparent order/accounting states.
  • Future independently verified matching, custody/wallet integration and reconciliation.
  • Separate application processes for asset listings; informational Silver Scan pages do not confer trading support.

These are separate current and future capabilities, not a claim that every function is already operational.

03 / GET STARTED

How to use or preview

Review the development terminal only as a simulation. Do not send real orders or funds to a local or preview address.

04 / PARTICIPATE

How to get involved

Matching-engine experts, ledger/accounting engineers, financial security auditors, operations engineers and trading UI designers can contribute.

Start from the repository's issues, documentation, test instructions or contribution process. Do not share private keys or production credentials.

05 / RELEASE READINESS

When will it be live?

No real-value exchange opening date announced; current interface is development-only.

Before a public readiness claim, these milestones require evidence:

  • Harden admin access, secrets, MFA, permissions and abuse prevention.
  • Implement and test matching, balances, deposits and withdrawals in isolated environments.
  • Independent controls, custody review and operational readiness before real-value trading.

Launch dates are not estimates unless published separately by the project. Repository activity and passing local tests do not establish safe public operation.

06 / ECOSYSTEM

Silver Labs integrations

Silver Scan research references, SilverPay connections and independently reviewed DEX markets.

Each integration needs separate compatibility, data-quality and security verification. A project being listed or featured does not mean wallets, pools or markets are live.
