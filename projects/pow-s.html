PoW-S

Transparent lockups and reward accounting across eligible networks.

Current development statusResearch / planned implementationSource-based overview · October 2026

PoW-S is a proposed lockup and reward program associated with future mining-pool fees and eligible assets. The scope is broader than BTCS: assets would require independent eligibility, network-aware accounting and appropriate legal and security review before real lockups begin.

View development source ↗Contribute or collaborate
01 / ENGINEERING

What has already been built

  • A separate research repository exists with project scope and design discussions.
  • A proposed Silver Pool fee model identifies a potential 1.5 percentage-point allocation from a future 2% fee.
  • No operational staking, payout contract, enforceable distribution service or live yield product is verified.
02 / PRODUCT

What the product is intended to provide

  • Optional defined lock terms, maturity displays and clear unlock rules.
  • Asset-specific accrual, reward history and independently auditable distribution statements.
  • Future cross-product visibility in Silver Pool, Silver Scan and SilvEx once validated.

These are separate current and future capabilities, not a claim that every function is already operational.

03 / GET STARTED

How to use or preview

There is no live PoW-S program to join or deposit into. Follow public design discussions; never send coins to an unverified lockup address.

04 / PARTICIPATE

How to get involved

Accounting designers, cryptographers, security auditors, economic modelers and legal/compliance reviewers can contribute to the proposal.

Start from the repository's issues, documentation, test instructions or contribution process. Do not share private keys or production credentials.

05 / RELEASE READINESS

When will it be live?

Not scheduled. Any return rates or rewards would be speculative until a funded, reviewed program exists.

Before a public readiness claim, these milestones require evidence:

  • Finalize eligibility, custody, reward funding and no-commingling requirements.
  • Test exact multi-asset balances, lock terms, distribution and failure recovery.
  • Obtain relevant legal/compliance and contract/security review before enabling funds movement.

Launch dates are not estimates unless published separately by the project. Repository activity and passing local tests do not establish safe public operation.

06 / ECOSYSTEM

Silver Labs integrations

Silver Pool fee allocation proposals, future SilvEx asset eligibility and Silver Scan reporting.

Each integration needs separate compatibility, data-quality and security verification. A project being listed or featured does not mean wallets, pools or markets are live.
